CADR Lab.

Enter room dimensions in meters, a published particle CADR in m³/h and an illustrative target eACH. We calculate room volume, simplified particle-equivalent air changes, and the CADR matching your selected target. No information leaves your browser.

Understand the model: rated CADR divided by room volume is a simplified particle eACH, not outdoor-air ventilation ACH. Ratings depend on test conditions, speed, positioning and mixing. The chosen target is not a universal code requirement or safety threshold. EPA’s separate area rule is also shown for comparison, assuming an 8-foot ceiling.

How to interpret the result

If the calculated required CADR is higher than the unit’s published rate, the simple target is not reached at that rating. If it is lower, the model does not prove the room is safe. Actual performance depends on the cleaner, pollutants, source control, runtime, airflow paths and filter maintenance. Use the result to compare specifications, not as a replacement for a trained ventilation assessment.
