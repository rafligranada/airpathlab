Room Pathfinder.

Pick the concern that best matches your situation. This tool suggests a cautious order of checks and links to the relevant guide; it cannot identify pollutants, give a medical diagnosis or certify a building.

Do not use this tool for emergencies. Suspected carbon monoxide, fuel-gas leakage, fire or sudden severe symptoms require appropriate emergency response. Leave an unsafe area and seek local emergency assistance; do not experiment with switches or indoor-air gadgets.
