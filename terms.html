Terms of use

Educational use, safety limits and accuracy terms for AirPath Lab.

Educational guidance only

AirPath Lab offers general information and simplified planning tools. We do not provide professional HVAC engineering, medical advice, air-quality certification, building inspection or equipment-repair services. Use of information is at your own discretion and must be checked against manufacturer instructions and applicable local requirements.

Safety first

Suspected fuel-gas leaks, carbon monoxide, fires, electrical hazards and acute severe symptoms need emergency or qualified professional response. Do not use our tools to justify entering a hazardous area, opening a sealed appliance, bypassing safety devices, changing ductwork or undertaking mold remediation without appropriate training.

Accuracy and availability

Specifications, standards, external sources and product capabilities may change. We strive to present clear assumptions but cannot guarantee the completeness, suitability or continuous availability of tools. A calculated eACH is not an on-site measurement. External links are provided as references, not endorsements.
