About AirPath Lab

About the independent project, audience, limitations and contact information.

What this project does

AirPath Lab is an independently maintained educational field guide about indoor airflow, practical source control, portable particle filtration and the everyday decisions that influence room comfort. We translate published guidance into plain-language checklists and openly explained calculations. We do not sell HVAC equipment or provide paid installation services.

Who it is for

The intended reader is a renter, homeowner or curious facility user who wants to understand the question to ask before buying an appliance. Each article starts with a specific problem and explains observations a reader can make without taking a system apart. We distinguish between ventilation, filtration and moisture control because confusing them often leads to ineffective purchases.

What we are not

AirPath Lab is not a certified HVAC engineering practice, environmental laboratory, medical provider or building inspection service. We have not performed field measurements in your home. Our estimates do not certify air safety, guarantee pollutant removal or establish compliance with local building and occupational requirements. Changes involving gas, electrical systems, ductwork, major water damage or suspected mold need qualified in-person help.

How to contact the publisher

We welcome reports of broken links, calculation mistakes, confusing explanations or accessibility issues. Reach the publisher at rafligranada@gmail.com. Please do not send confidential health records, complete addresses or account passwords. Read our editorial standards and primary source directory.
