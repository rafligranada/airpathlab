Privacy policy

How AirPath Lab handles browser storage, analytics-related choices and third-party advertising.

Scope and contact

AirPath Lab is a static informational website. Contact: rafligranada@gmail.com. This notice describes the website files as provided; hosting infrastructure and optional connected services may independently process technical request information.

Information you choose to provide

When you email the publisher, your email provider transmits your address and message. Do not send confidential personal information. Calculations in our CADR Lab and Room Pathfinder occur locally in your browser and are not sent to AirPath Lab by our application code.

Local storage

The Filter Service Log stores entries in your browser’s localStorage under a site-specific key. You can remove entries within the tool or clear browser site data. Records are not synchronized across devices, and losing browser storage may erase them. CSV exports are created in the browser, and you control where they are saved.

We include Google AdSense script on the homepage and editorial guide pages. If ads are activated, Google and its partners may process information, store or access cookies or use similar technologies for ad delivery, personalization subject to applicable law and settings. Review Google advertising policies and Google privacy policy for details. AdSense approval and ads appearing are not guaranteed.

Choice and changes

You can use browser privacy controls, delete local site data and adjust Google ad settings where available. If the site later adds analytics, forms or other services, this notice must be updated to reflect actual practices. Policy effective 12 November 2026.
