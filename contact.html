What to include

If reporting an issue, please include the page URL, the sentence or feature involved, and a concise description of what should be corrected. If it concerns a calculator, describe the inputs and expected result without including identifying personal information.

What we cannot provide

We cannot diagnose a hazardous building condition, estimate individual medical risk or offer urgent responses to gas, carbon monoxide, electrical or fire emergencies. Use local emergency resources and qualified professionals for those situations. Please do not submit private records, equipment access credentials or sensitive health details by email.
