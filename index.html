WORKED EXAMPLE / PARTICLE CLEANING

250 ÷ 50 = 5

A rated 250 m³/h CADR in a 50 m³ room corresponds to an estimated 5 particle-equivalent air changes per hour in simplified math. That is not 5 fresh-air changes and not a guarantee of actual performance.

  • 01Know the room volume
  • 02Use a published particle CADR
  • 03Check speed, placement and runtime
THE AIRPATH METHOD

Source. Ventilate. Filter.

EPA guidance prioritizes controlling pollution sources and ventilating with suitable outdoor air. Filtration can supplement that strategy, not replace it. There is no single device that solves moisture, gases and particles alike.

Read the three-part method ↗
  • 01Remove or reduce the source when possible
  • 02Ventilate when outdoor conditions and building safety allow
  • 03Use suitably sized filtration for relevant particles
  • 04Re-check the assumptions and get professional advice when needed
